# Management tools and optional expansion

These additions are original remake features. They do not change the recovered IdleCorp rules by default. `world.expansionEnabled` defaults to `false`; an administrator can enable it without replacing the existing world. Rules and limits are defined in `packages/rules/src/expansion.ts` and returned by `/api/content` and account snapshots.

Facility groups, favorites, batch pause/resume, regional production goals, market watchlists, notification read state and sampled histories persist with the corporation. All mutation actions use the existing PostgreSQL transaction lock and payload-bound idempotency receipts. Facility selection is ownership-checked before batch edits; resumed facilities begin a new complete cycle.

Facility utilization counts observed successful, input-starved and capacity-blocked cycles. Paused time is excluded. Margin estimates use ordinary output NPC sell quotes minus input NPC buy replacement costs; they are not realized profit and exclude quality bonuses, retail and player orders. Corporation histories sample actual saved balances at most once per minute, retaining 720 points. Watched-asset quotes sample once per hour per region, retaining 168 points per series. No historical data is invented after downtime. Reset actions force an additional balance sample. Return summaries compare real counters and inventory with the previous account snapshot.

Supply contracts require logistics at both endpoints. The buyer escrows quantity times price; another corporation accepts one supply origin. Each partial delivery consumes real supplier goods, reduces buyer escrow, and places goods and payment in each party's inbox. Current expansion contracts have no fee. Buyer or supplier may cancel the undelivered remainder; the buyer receives only the unused escrow. Deadline expiry has the same refund behavior. Claims cannot replay. Refunds remain claimable when wallet or inventory capacity prevents immediate collection.

Regional projects pool exact configured cash and material requirements. Contributions remain in escrow and count toward the contributor's net worth until completion. Contributors may withdraw while funding; proposers may cancel and refund everyone. Expiry refunds all contributions. Completed contributions are consumed as an explicit economic sink, providing a bounded regional benefit for the documented duration. Production and happiness benefit expiry joins the chronological simulation event stream. Export benefits affect newly dispatched trips and preserve their saved arrival times.

Disabling expansion prevents new contracts, contract acceptance, projects and contributions. Existing delivery, cancellation, withdrawal, expiry and claim operations remain available; completed benefits expire normally.

Liquidation cancels contracts associated with the selected region and withdraws that corporation's local project contributions. Cash refunds remain in the global inbox; withdrawn local project materials enter the normal liquidation value once. Reincorporation cancels all of the corporation's commitments before its ordinary balances reset. Counterpart corporations keep completed deliveries and earned payments and receive remaining refunds. Other project contributors remain invested. Completed regional projects survive corporation resets until their scheduled expiry.

Regression coverage: `apps/server/test/enhancements.test.ts` exercises ownership, pause/resume deadlines, real metrics and sampling, fee quotes, partial delivery conservation, disabled-mode settlement, expiry at numeric capacity, pooled contributions, benefit catch-up equivalence, and reset interactions with outstanding contracts/projects.
